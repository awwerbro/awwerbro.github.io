Our article [**"Fast, In Situ Gas Analysis during Atomic Layer Deposition through Optical Emission Spectroscopy and Non-Negative Matrix Factorization"**](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acssensors.5c01731) has been published (with Philippe Smet, Dirk Poelman, Jolien Dendooven and Christophe Detavernier - thank you!) 

We modified a [Gencoa OPTIX](https://www.gencoa.com/optix) sensor to probe gas composition during ALD, and show that non-negative matrix factorization (NMF)-based analysis is superior over naive tracking of emission lines: there are plenty of spurious peaks and some nasty side-effects when you use an electrical discharge to continuously probe gas composition during ALD. This is another application where this NMF really shines, as it neatly solves these issues.
