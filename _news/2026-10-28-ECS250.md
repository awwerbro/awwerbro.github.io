I got invited to present the LLM discovery work at the ALD symposium of the ECS 250 conference in Calgary, Canada. Despite the complex story (and several iterations of the presentation to get it right) I believe I got the main point across: discovery is more than optimization, and LLM agents are somewhat decent at it. You can find the [**abstract here**](https://ecs.confex.com/ecs/250/meetingapp.cgi/Paper/220302).

Apart from my own talk, I thought it was a great conference. I learned a lot about other peoples research, and I definitely enjoyed the networking part, too. Thanks to the committee for the invitation, and hopefully until soon!
