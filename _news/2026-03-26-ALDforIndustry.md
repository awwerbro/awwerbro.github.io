Last week, I traveled to Eindhoven (NL) to talk about anomaly detection using quadrupole mass spectrometry at the [ALD for industry Conference](https://efds.org/en/event/ald-for-industry-2026/) (thanks to [Hiden Analytical](https://www.hidenanalytical.com/) for the invitation!). It was great catching up with people, discussing presentations, and getting to know the industry side of ALD a little better.

Some takeaways from my presentation  

🖇️ **The surface drives the gas phase** - or was it the other way around? 

🤷 **unknown unknowns are real**: just like with reaction products, you can not always predict where anomalies will appear. Measuring the full picture of gas in your reactor is extremely helpful to quickly detect them.

🥛 you only need to **measure one half cycle** to detect anomalies. Anomalies in the other half-cycle will inevitably impact the one you're measuring. This is great for the lifetime of your spectrometer!
