Last week, professor Matthew Maschmann passed away. I first got to know Matt in January 2023. I was visiting MU with the idea of joining as a postdoc. We had dinner together the first night of my visit. I remember being struck by how down to earth, approachable and honest he was.

When I joined MUMSEI later that year, he remained a mentor and a role model. Scientific vision paired with lightheartedness is a rare combination, and he embodied both. My stay at MU changed my perspective on life and careers. Matt played a big role in that. He showed me what mentorship could look like. Even now I'm back home in Belgium, the opportunities he offered and things he made possible continue to shape my work and life. 

The news of his illness and passing has deeply saddened me, and my thoughts are with his family and friends. I will never forget him.
