Matthias presented our work on fast analysis of 4D-STEM datasets today in Charlotte, NC, at the [AVS conference](https://avs71.avs.org/). The presentation was titled "Data Science Tools to Disentangle Large Electron Diffraction Datasets of Thin Films" and contained the final RNMF story, but also new insights about using superpixels to compress 2D diffraction data without meaningful loss of data.
