I recently met [Mariana Fazio](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fn8GTZUAAAAJ), a UK postdoc working on gravitational wave detectors, but also very interested in using AI and machine learning for experimental science - something that is close to my heart as well. After two meetings we figured out we had too much scientific interest in common to leave it there, so we decided to start a "data club" focused on using machine learning and AI in a "real" lab, with "real" data. The idea is to meet every other monday (online) around lunchtime, to discuss fresh papers, methods, etc. You can find all info (and how to sign up for the mailinglist) in our [Github repository](https://github.com/awwerbro/materials_and_ml)
