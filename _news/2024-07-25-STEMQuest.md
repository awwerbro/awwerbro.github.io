As a part of the STEMQuest summer camp, [Dibya Kanti Datta](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=88PwyYQAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) and I taught a day of chemical kinetics to high school and middle school students who aspire to become engineers. 

To get everyone at the same page, we started by reviewing the basics, balancing equations. The topic of kinetics was introduced using a variety of real-world examples: from cooking a steak over catalytic converters to mold growth. The students used jupyter notebooks to visualize how different parameters affect the reaction. You can test this notebook out yourself [here](https://colab.research.google.com/github/awwerbro/outreach/blob/main/kinetics.ipynb)

Dibya and I smuggled our research in the session as well: the students got hands-on experience reacting CaCO3 and vinegar, competed to make the most efficient water filters (Dibya works on water quality), and 'wrote with light' making their own cyanotype imprints (linking lithography and photograpy). 

Read more about STEMquest [here](https://engineering.missouri.edu/2024/k-12-students-explore-stem-opportunities-at-mizzou-engineering-camps/)
