I am currently a [MUMSEI](https://msei.missouri.edu/) postdoctoral researcher at the university of Missouri where I am working with prof. [Matthias Young](https://younggroup.missouri.edu/) and prof. [Matthew Maschmann](https://engineering.missouri.edu/faculty/matt-maschmann/) (September '23-September '25). Previously I worked at Stanford University in the group of prof. [Stacey Bent](https://bentgroup.stanford.edu/) and I finished my PhD in the [Cocoon research group](https://www.ugent.be/we/solidstatesciences/cocoon/en) (Belgium) with prof. [Christophe Detavernier](https://biblio.ugent.be/person/801001185275) and prof. [Jolien Dendooven](https://scholar.google.nl/citations?user=Bq6Big0AAAAJ&hl=nl).

I believe automated data collection and computer-driven analysis have tremendous opportunity and are vastly underused by materials scientists. I envision to design, build and use **in situ and operando platforms** to collect large datasets on thin film formation, surface evolution, and surface modification for various applications. **Computer-assisted data-reduction strategies**, combined with **modeling** approaches can allow to extract meaningful information from these datasets.
