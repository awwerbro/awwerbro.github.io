Andreas Werbrouck

I am currently a MUMSEI postdoctoral researcher at the University of Missouri where I am working with prof. Matthias Young and prof. Matthew Maschmann (September ‘23-September ‘25). Previously I worked at Stanford University in the group of prof. Stacey Bent. I finished my PhD in the Cocoon research group (Belgium) with prof. Christophe Detavernier and prof. Jolien Dendooven.

I believe automated data collection and computer-driven analysis have tremendous opportunity and are vastly underused by materials scientists. I designed, built and used in situ and operando platforms to collect large datasets on thin film formation, surface evolution, and interface modification for various applications. Real-world data, combined with modeling and AI/ML strategies will allow us to see complex materials problems in a new light.

And yep, I’m a white guy and I confirm all prevailing stereotypes about academics. However, I think that we (‘the world’) miss out on talent and perspective if only people who look like me are seen in science. Ideas get stronger when they get challenged, and we’ll need everyone on board if we are to face the future confidently.
