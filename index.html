I am currently a MUMSEI postdoctoral researcher at the university of Missouri where I am working with prof. Matthias Young and prof. Matthew Maschmann (September ‘23-September ‘25). Previously I worked at Stanford University in the group of prof. Stacey Bent and I finished my PhD in the Cocoon research group (Belgium) with prof. Christophe Detavernier and prof. Jolien Dendooven.

I believe automated data collection and computer-driven analysis have tremendous opportunity and are vastly underused by materials scientists. I designed, built and used in situ and operando platforms to collect large datasets on thin film formation, surface evolution, and interface modification for various applications. Computer-assisted data-reduction strategies, combined with modeling approaches can allow to extract meaningful information from these systems.
